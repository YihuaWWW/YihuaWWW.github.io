**PhD Candidate | School of Mathematics, Shandong University | Operations Research and Control Theory**

Yihua Wang is a PhD candidate in the School of Mathematics at Shandong University, where she began her doctoral studies in 2021 under the supervision of Bingqiang Liu. Previously, she worked as a research assistant in the Microecology Laboratory at the School of Stomatology, Shandong University (2018–2021) under PI Qiang Feng.  

Her research focuses on bioinformatics and multi-omics studies of the human microbiome. She is dedicated to addressing computational challenges in large-scale microbiome multi-omics data processing and analysis by integrating graph theory, statistics, combinatorial optimization, and graph-based deep learning models. Dr. Wang aims to develop data- and intelligence-driven cutting-edge omics technologies to explore the structural composition and dynamic patterns of microbiota across different human body sites and elucidate their relationships with human health. She has published over ten research papers and patents and serves as a reviewer for journals including *Computational Biology and Chemistry* and *Frontiers*.
